# Content research for SGA

Find a source worth adapting into an original Sumber Griya Abadi (SGA) carousel. Preserve the source's *mechanism*—the audience tension, hook, pacing, and payoff—while handing off enough evidence to create a distinct post.

## 1. Find candidates

1. Establish the topic, target audience, and any product or campaign focus from the request. Use supplied SGA materials or its official channels to confirm the business and audience context. If that context is still unclear, state the assumption or ask for the missing focus; do not invent products, offers, or expertise.
2. Use the available ChatGPT/Codex browser with the user's connected X (Twitter) and Threads sessions. Search several topic terms, common audience problems, and adjacent phrases. Use platform search and public web results where available. If sign-in or a connection is required, ask the user to complete it; never ask for or handle a password. Record any platform or access limits.
3. Read the full text, thread, and attached slides or media for each promising post. Save its URL, creator, date, visible engagement counts, and observation time. Note when counts or parts of a post are unavailable.
4. Shortlist posts with both audience relevance and unusual engagement. Compare posts of similar age and creator size when possible; a large raw like count by itself is weak evidence. Look for replies, reposts, saves when visible, and discussion quality. Separate observed engagement from your inference about why it worked.

## 2. Evaluate with Jev

Use TypeSafe's Jev Score questions to judge the *adaptation opportunity*, not to claim a literal probability of going viral. Read [Jev scoring](references/jev-scoring.md) before the first scoring run. Include the original post, observed engagement, audience context, and a brief proposed SGA angle in the state. Use the same rubric for all candidates and show the score, confidence, and evidence. Check whether the subject can be adapted accurately and originally for SGA.

Select a source when its composite opportunity score reaches **75/100**, SGA fit reaches **1.5/2**, and the factual premise can be verified. If none qualifies, search another angle or report the best candidates with the reason no source passed. If Jev access is unavailable, give a clearly labeled qualitative shortlist and leave Jev scores blank; do not present a guessed number as a Jev result.

## 3. Handoff

For the selected source, provide a compact brief containing:

- Source URL, creator, date, a faithful synopsis of every source slide or thread segment, and visible engagement evidence. Quote only short excerpts needed to identify the hook.
- The hook, audience tension, emotional or practical payoff, and progression that made it work.
- Jev dimension scores, composite score, confidence, and any uncertainty or missing metrics.
- A distinct SGA angle, claims requiring verification, and the elements that must be rewritten or redesigned to make the carousel original.

When the request includes making the carousel, continue with the sibling [`create-carousel-post`](../create-carousel-post/SKILL.md) skill using this brief. Stop after the research brief when the user asked only for research.
